# External panel sources

Accessed 2026-11-13. Raw files are snapshots; source pages and files may be revised.

## Argentina — INDEC supermarket sales, non-tax panel

- Publisher: Instituto Nacional de Estadística y Censos (INDEC), Dirección Nacional de Estadísticas Económicas, Dirección de Estadísticas de Comercio y Servicios, *Encuesta de supermercados*. Source workbook: https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/economia/serie_supermercados.xlsx, saved as `super/serie_supermercados.xlsx` on 2026-11-13. SHA-256: `112cf5646feb5a94a050ebf9c0aab62594c37414261386041ae75f5701df1e85`. Workbook metadata last modified 2026-10-21.
- Source sheet: `Cuadro 5.`, *Ventas totales a precios corrientes por grupo de artículos según jurisdicción, en miles de pesos*. January 2017–August 2026, 116 monthly observations; 25 geographic areas including CABA, 24 Greater Buenos Aires partidos, and the rest of Buenos Aires, crossed with 11 product groups. `Cuadro 3.` supplies an independent national total check. Original unit: **thousands of current Argentine pesos**, including VAT. `pilots/ext_super_data.py` multiplies by 1,000 to produce nominal ARS in `data/processed/ext_super_panel.parquet`. The `tax` column names a product group for compatibility; these are retail sales, not taxes.
- Six area × product cells contain `s` in every month: two each for Formosa, Santa Cruz, and Tierra del Fuego. The parser does not infer confidential cells. It uses each published area total less its disclosed groups as one `SUPPRESSED_REMAINDER` group for that area. The resulting 272 monthly series sum to the national total, but the three suppressed product categories cannot be completely allocated to areas. See `research/codex_ext_super.md`.
- Terms: [INDEC's website](https://www.indec.gob.ar/indec/web/Nivel4-Tema-3-1-34) states that all material on the site is [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) unless specifically marked otherwise; the workbook has no visible exception. Reuse requires credit, a licence link, disclosure of changes, and ShareAlike for adapted material. Attribution: “Source: INDEC, Encuesta de supermercados, Cuadro 5, accessed 2026-11-13; transformed to ARS and combined suppressed cells into area remainders.” No INDEC endorsement is implied.
- Methodology: [INDEC Methodology No. 30](https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/economia/metodologia30_supermercados_mayoristas.pdf) describes the 2017 expansion of the company panel, the 11 groups, inclusion of VAT, and a nonprobability company panel. Its [December 2022 update](https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/economia/actualizacion_metodologica_supermercados_mayoristas.pdf) changes seasonal adjustment of index series, not the original current-price sales used here. This workbook is a revised snapshot, not a set of historical release vintages.
- Optional CPI: `super/serie_ipc_aperturas.csv` is INDEC's [historical regional CPI apertures CSV](https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/economia/serie_ipc_aperturas.csv), downloaded 2026-11-13, SHA-256 `76387fe7fe3b4a53a72e5359bf0fe0b4ea966af76bf6f0c14563e078fb2030b0`. Its `Nivel general y divisiones COICOP` records are extracted by `pilots/ext_super_cpi.py` into `super/cpi_region_divisions.csv` (SHA-256 `7c8f909a0c14ea94e565e1389cb2b7e4ca481c04950bbff57a246909e3300562`): 6 regions × 13 headline/division categories × 119 months, December 2016–October 2026, index base December 2016 = 100. `super/cpi_area_region_map.csv` maps all 25 sales areas to these six regions according to [INDEC's regional definitions](https://www.indec.gob.ar/ftp/cuadros/poblacion/manual_estilo_indec_07_17.pdf). The same INDEC site CC BY-SA 4.0 notice applies absent an exception. These regional/division CPI indexes are price proxies, not a one-to-one match to supermarket product groups; do not join realized future CPI into a forecast origin.

## Argentina — chosen panel

- Publisher: Argentina Ministerio de Economía, Subsecretaría de Coordinación Fiscal Provincial, *Recursos Tributarios de Origen Provincial (TOP)*. [Monthly source page](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/economia/sechacienda/asuntosprovinciales/direccion-de-recursos-provinciales/recursos-tributarios-de-origen-provincial-top).
- License: [Argentina.gob.ar terms](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/terminos-y-condiciones), section *Propiedad intelectual*, license site contents under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** unless stated otherwise. The TOP page/workbooks state no exception. Research use and adapted data are permitted with attribution. Credit: Ministerio de Economía, Argentina, provincial tax administrations, TOP monthly workbooks; accessed 2026-11-13.
- Coverage: January 2017–December 2025, monthly, 23 provinces plus C.A.B.A., five categories (IIBB, INMOBILIARIO, SELLOS, AUTOMOTORES, OTROS). Files contain one sheet per category and a separate TOTAL sheet. Original unit: **millions of current Argentine pesos**; panel converts to nominal ARS by multiplying by 1,000,000.

- Caveats: Monthly figures replicate the provincial administrations' releases without federal harmonization. The publisher warns that reporting frequency can vary, that some administrations include non-tax receipts, and that its separate quarterly/annual series uses a different universe and need not equal the monthly series. Some workbook zeros represent no provincial report: Corrientes, La Pampa, and Santiago del Estero have zero IIBB and other categories throughout 2017–2019, so their series fail the `kept` filter. Do not infer that those provinces collected no tax. The five categories and definitions belong to *provincial-origin* taxes, not national ARCA collections. The 2025 workbook itself shows last update 2026-09-14.

## Inspection files for other candidates

- **Colombia DIAN:** `colombia/recaudo_seccionales_2005_2026.xlsx` from https://www.dian.gov.co/dian/cifras/EstadisticasRecaudo/Recaudo-bruto-por-seccionales-y-tipo-de-impuesto-2005-2026.xlsx. Accessed 2026-11-13. The workbook is by seccional and tax but has **annual** observations only (2005–2026p), so it cannot supply the required monthly/quarterly panel. Unit: millions of current Colombian pesos. [DIAN source page](https://www.dian.gov.co/dian/cifras/Paginas/EstadisticasRecaudo.aspx). No research-use license determination was needed for this rejected file.
- **India GST:** `india/gst_dec_2024.pdf` from https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/approved_monthly_gst_data_for_publishing_dec_2024.pdf. Accessed 2026-11-13. Its state-by-CGST/SGST/IGST/cess table covers **April–December 2024 cumulative** amounts, in crore rupees, rather than a ready eight-year monthly state-by-tax series. The contemporaneous monthly state table reports total GST only. An April 2025 PDF request returned HTTP 503 from this machine. [GST Council data page](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/gst) exposes national monthly totals. No license determination was needed for this rejected sample.

## Argentina — BCRA REM expectations

- Publisher: Banco Central de la República Argentina (BCRA), *Relevamiento de Expectativas de Mercado (REM)*. Accessed 2026-11-13. The historical workbook is a snapshot through the October 2026 survey; the parser does not download or update files at runtime.
- `rem/relevamiento-expectativas-mercado-historico.xlsx`: https://www.bcra.gob.ar/archivos/Pdfs/PublicacionesEstadisticas/informes/relevamiento-expectativas-mercado-historico.xlsx, linked from https://www.bcra.gob.ar/relevamiento-expectativas-mercado-rem/. SHA-256: `4e759899dec322f3cc01bd2db3cc89a248c7c5e201a88bde860a7453cf18df9b`.
- `rem/publicaciones_rem.json`: BCRA publication listing snapshot, https://www.bcra.gob.ar/wp-json/bcra/v1/publicaciones?category=rem&lang=es&action=total, linked through the BCRA [REM archive](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/todos-relevamiento-de-expectativas-de-mercado-rem/). Its `fecha` can reflect site posting or migration rather than the original report release.
- `rem/publication_dates_evidence.json`: saved extraction of the archive entries and dates explicitly stated in their linked BCRA report pages, with report URLs. This is a derived provenance snapshot, not another independent dataset. Three unresolved publication dates use a documented estimate in the parser.
- `rem/REM180131_Resultados_web.pdf`: original January 2018 report, https://www.bcra.gob.ar/archivos/Pdfs/PublicacionesEstadisticas/REM180131%20Resultados%20web.pdf. It resolves the historical workbook's duplicated June 2018 CPI target: the second row is July 2018.
- Terms: [BCRA legal notice](https://www.bcra.gob.ar/aviso-legal/), section 3, permits reuse for informational, educational, or academic purposes with full BCRA attribution, faithful context, and no misleading edits. It prohibits commercial, advertising, or campaign use without prior express authorization. No Creative Commons or open-data license is asserted for these files. The medians are market participants' forecasts, not BCRA's own projections.

## Peru — SUNAT candidate (built; reuse licence unresolved)

- Publisher: SUNAT, [Nota Tributaria, ingresos recaudados](https://www.sunat.gob.pe/estadisticasestudios/ingresos-recaudados.html), accessed 2026-11-13. `peru/cdro_A15.xlsx` is [Cuadro A15](https://www.sunat.gob.pe/estadisticasestudios/nota_tributaria/cdro_A15.xlsx), monthly region × internal-tax data, January 2005–December 2025 complete (the workbook also has preliminary 2026 months). Original unit: **thousands of soles**. SHA-256: `aaf424884834b133334881e6e034a5e53f0a69958f068447d343fab7085a3a03`.
- `peru/cdro_A13.xlsx` is [Cuadro A13](https://www.sunat.gob.pe/estadisticasestudios/nota_tributaria/cdro_A13.xlsx), official national internal-tax total by month, for reconciliation. Its cells are in **soles** despite a title that says millions. SHA-256: `484b2a0b577a0c904f1bedbdc4080d506a6727f11e03f95fb5e4f40328c2c73d`.
- `peru/cdro_A3.xlsx` is [Cuadro A3](https://www.sunat.gob.pe/estadisticasestudios/nota_tributaria/cdro_A3.xlsx), national internal-tax category detail. SHA-256: `bc0b66f351396a3bcaa0c4d6baefcd17f9a8e57474c7a70584132427acf42094`.
- `peru_cpi.json` is BCRP's [monthly Lima Metropolitana CPI index, December 2021 = 100](https://estadisticas.bcrp.gob.pe/estadisticas/series/api/PN38705PM/json/2005-1/2025-12), originally produced by INEI, 252 months. SHA-256: `f1d9182fb27a3e756768fb7887c7ef117160d6c7a88138d944f85fb05e757b93`. The BCRP API required `curl -k` from this machine because its certificate chain failed local verification; the dataset identity was checked against the [BCRP series page](https://estadisticas.bcrp.gob.pe/estadisticas/series/mensuales/resultados/PN38705PM/html/2020-1/2025-6/).
- `peru/eem_series.json` is BCRP's [monthly inflation and GDP expectations series](https://estadisticas.bcrp.gob.pe/estadisticas/series/api/PD12912AM-PD38048AM/json/2005-1/2025-12), 252 survey-month records. SHA-256: `8de4202030e900e60bdfc78f9d73acab83e6895cfeb3913e627f7fb9375be835`. This API snapshot **does not supply publication dates or historical vintages** and must not be treated as a point-in-time information set. Dated [survey reports](https://www.bcrp.gob.pe/docs/Publicaciones/Notas-Estudios/2025/nota-de-estudios-57-2025.pdf) exist, but their PDF downloads returned an Incapsula HTML block to `curl` here.
- Attribution: A15's internal taxes are allocated by **taxpayer fiscal domicile**, not by location of economic activity. The A15 notes explicitly describe Lima concentration for financial institutions. No explicit reuse licence was found on the A15 source page or workbook. Peru's [copyright statute](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/pcm/normas-legales/1670023-822) excludes simple facts/data from copyright protection, but that is **not** an affirmative licence for this specific compiled workbook. The separate SUNAT datasets on Peru's open-data portal have ODC-BY labels; those labels cannot be transferred to A15. Therefore the Peru candidate is retained for analysis but not certified against the brief's licence requirement.

## Spain — chosen licensed panel

- Publisher: Agencia Estatal de Administración Tributaria (AEAT), [monthly tax-receipts release](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/datosabiertos/catalogo/hacienda/Informe_mensual_de_Recaudacion_Tributaria.shtml). `spain/ingresos_por_delegaciones.xlsx` is [Ingresos por delegaciones](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Tema/Estadisticas/Recaudacion_Tributaria/Informes_mensuales/Ingresos_por_Delegaciones.xlsx), downloaded 2026-11-13; release page stated updated 2026-10-30. Original unit: **thousands of euros**; its `datos_delegaciones` sheet contains 56 territorial delegations, central services, four major tax types, and national totals for January 2007–December 2025 plus partial 2026. SHA-256: `88f812a02a817f0f74356c7fbd6f42d0a6a624619c17af6c7dae86624abe0ac9`.
- `spain/cuadros_estadisticos_series.xlsx` is the companion [Cuadros estadísticos y series](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Tema/Estadisticas/Recaudacion_Tributaria/Informes_mensuales/Cuadros_estadisticos_series_es_es.xlsx), downloaded for inspection. SHA-256: `e8c74aee86c3adaf9ca0dc12be186dd87f3affb0a310bb6a64a7f28d16dc6f4a`.
- Reuse terms: [AEAT's conditions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/gobierno-abierto/reutilizacion-informacion/condiciones-reutilizacion.html) permit reuse with source credit, preserving meaning and available update metadata, and without implying endorsement. [AEAT's own description](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/informacion-institucional/memorias/memoria-2024/4-principales-actuaciones-2024/4_1-prevencion-fraude-tributario-aduanero/4_1_7-reutilizacion-informacion.html) expressly states commercial and noncommercial reuse is authorized unless otherwise indicated. No exception is indicated for this workbook. Credit: AEAT, Informe Mensual de Recaudación Tributaria, Ingresos por delegaciones; updated 2026-10-30, accessed 2026-11-13. This is a public-sector reuse permission, not a Creative Commons label.
- `spain_cpi.csv` is INE's [national general CPI index, monthly table 24077](https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/files/t/csv_bdsc/24077.csv), downloaded 2026-11-13. It contains 2007–2025 complete monthly coverage and earlier/later months. Original CSV uses `;` delimiters and comma decimals. SHA-256: `0eb4a6e0d8c5e5a775eb4902d181a1480fafcee63f395afdaa0b5444d103052f`. [INE catalog record](https://datos.gob.es/es/catalogo/ea0042823-indice-general-nacional-series-desde-enero-de-1961-ipc-identificador-api-24077).
- Caveats: AEAT receipts are net cash collections after refunds, so negative monthly cells are genuine and common. Delegations follow the taxpayer's tax domicile; central services are an accounting entity, not a region. The workbook covers the *territorio de régimen fiscal común* and is not a complete map of all taxes collected by Spain's foral administrations. Four selected taxes do not equal the all-tax national total. See [AEAT explanatory notes](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/static_files/Sede/Tema/Estadisticas/Recaudacion_Tributaria/Informes_mensuales/Notas_explicativas_es_es.pdf).
